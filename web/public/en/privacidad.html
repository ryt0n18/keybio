Privacy and legal notice

Last updated: 24 October 2026.

This translation is provided for convenience. If there is any discrepancy, the Spanish version prevails.

Data controller

This website and the KeyBio program are a personal project by . For any question about your data you can write through the contact form.

The demo does not send your keystrokes

The way you type is biometric data, so the demo is designed not to collect it: the timing of your keystrokes is processed in your browser and stored only there (local storage), so that you can come back to the demo later. It is never sent to this website or to third parties. You can delete it at any time with the “Delete all data” button in the demo or by clearing the site data in your browser.

What data is processed

  • Ratings: the stars and, if you write them, a name and a comment. To prevent abuse, an encrypted fingerprint (HMAC) of your IP address is stored, not the IP itself; it is only used to limit ratings to one per day. The anti-spam check is done by Cloudflare Turnstile.
  • Contact: your name, your email address and your message, which are forwarded by email through the Web3Forms service so that I can reply to you.
  • Preferences: the light or dark theme and the language you choose are stored in your browser.

The website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. To count visits it uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and does not identify anyone: it only gives totals (pages viewed, country, browser and referring website).

Purposes and legal basis

  • Publishing the ratings and the website's average, with your consent when you send them.
  • Replying to your messages, with your consent when you send the form.
  • Protecting the website against spam and abuse (legitimate interest).

How long it is kept

Ratings are kept while the website is active or until you ask for them to be deleted. Contact messages, for as long as needed to deal with them.

Service providers

  • Cloudflare: website hosting, the ratings database (stored in the European Union), anti-spam check (Turnstile) and cookie-free visit counts (Web Analytics).
  • Web3Forms: forwarding contact form messages to the owner's email.
  • hCaptcha (Intuition Machines): anti-spam check on the contact form. It only loads when you start typing in it.

These providers may process data outside the European Economic Area with the safeguards provided for in the General Data Protection Regulation.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, object to its processing or withdraw your consent by writing through the contact form. If you believe your rights have not been respected, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD) or to the data protection authority in your country.

The downloadable program

KeyBio keeps profiles and history only on the computer where it is installed (optionally encrypted with a password) and does not send data over the internet. If you use it with other people, ask for their permission before enrolling the way they type.
